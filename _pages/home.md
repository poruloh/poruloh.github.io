## Welcome to the Loh Laboratory

We develop and apply computational methods to study how inherited and acquired genetic variants influence human health and disease.

We are based in the [Division of Genetics][bwhgenetics] and [Center for Data Sciences][bwhcds] at [Brigham and Women's Hospital][bwh] (part of [Mass General Brigham][mgb]) and [Harvard Medical School][hms]. We are affiliated with the Program in Medical and Population Genetics at the [Broad Institute][broad].

Our work has been generously supported by an NIH Director's New Innovator Award, a Burroughs Wellcome Fund Career Award at the Scientific Interface, a Glenn Foundation for Medical Research and AFAR Grant for Junior Faculty, and a Sloan Research Fellowship.
